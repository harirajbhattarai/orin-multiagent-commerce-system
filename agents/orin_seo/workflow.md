# ORIN SEO — Workflow

For every SEO task:

1. Identify page/topic type.
2. Identify search intent.
3. Define primary keyword.
4. Suggest secondary keywords.
5. Suggest long-tail keywords.
6. Recommend page/content structure.
7. Suggest internal links.
8. Suggest FAQ opportunities.
9. Suggest schema if relevant.
10. Provide meta title.
11. Provide meta description.
12. Give next actions.
