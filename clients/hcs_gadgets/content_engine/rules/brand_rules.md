# HCS Gadgets Brand Rules

## Client
HCS Gadgets

## Website
hcsgadgets.com

## Business Type
Multi-category ecommerce marketplace.

## Brand Positioning
HCS Gadgets is a practical online marketplace for useful home, lifestyle, gadget, garden, seasonal, and everyday products.

## Content Goal
Create helpful SEO blog content that supports product discovery, improves trust, and guides customers toward relevant products without sounding overly promotional.

## Tone of Voice
- Clear
- Helpful
- Practical
- Buyer-friendly
- Trust-focused
- Simple UK English
- Not overly luxury
- Not too playful
- Not robotic

## Writing Style
Articles should feel like useful shopping guides, checklists, comparisons, and product education pieces.

Avoid:
- exaggerated claims
- fake expert claims
- copied competitor wording
- medical/health claims unless fully safe
- warranty, delivery, or pricing claims unless verified

## CTA Style
Soft commercial CTA only.

Examples:
- Explore practical home and lifestyle products at HCS Gadgets.
- Browse useful everyday gadgets and home essentials.
- Find practical products for your home, garden, travel, and daily routine.

## Internal Linking Focus
Link to relevant HCS Gadgets collections, products, and supporting blog articles only when useful.

## Safety Rule
No article should be pushed live automatically. Shopify draft only after checks.
