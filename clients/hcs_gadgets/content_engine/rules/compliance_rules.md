# HCS Gadgets Compliance Rules

## Main Safety Rule
No article should publish live automatically.

Allowed:
- local HTML draft
- Shopify unpublished draft after checks
- Obsidian export
- GitHub backup

Not allowed:
- live Shopify publishing without human approval
- unsupported delivery claims
- unsupported warranty claims
- unsupported pricing claims
- fake reviews or fake testimonials
- medical or health claims unless verified and safe

## Product Claims
Only mention product features that are confirmed.

Avoid:
- waterproof unless verified
- safest, best, or number one unless verified
- cures, treats, heals, or prevents
- guaranteed results
- official certification claims unless confirmed

## Marketplace Risk
Because HCS Gadgets is multi-category, each article must be checked by category.

Examples:
- BBQ and garden content: avoid fire safety guarantees.
- Electronics and gadgets: avoid battery or safety claims unless verified.
- Home products: avoid durability guarantees unless verified.
- Aroma and fragrance products: avoid medical wellbeing claims.
