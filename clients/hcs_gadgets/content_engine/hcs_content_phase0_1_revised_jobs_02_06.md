## Job 04 — Rename Before Writing

**Current topic:** Useful Gadgets That Are Actually Worth Buying
**Proposed topic:** The Best Practical Everyday Gadgets Under £50 for UK Households
**Target date:** 2026-07-27
**Expected draft date:** 2026-07-13
**Cluster:** Gadgets & Useful Finds
**Target keyword:** practical everyday gadgets under 50 pounds

**Why rename:**
- Current title too close to Job 01 "Useful Home Gadgets That Make Daily Life Easier"
- Both share: "Useful" keyword, practical intent, general gadget angle
- Google may classify these as near-duplicate articles
- "That Are Actually Worth Buying" is vague and could apply to any product article

**What changes:**
- "Under £50" adds a specific price scope that Job 01 doesn't have
- "Practical Everyday" differentiates from "Home" in Job 01 — one is price-scoped, one is room-scoped
- "The Best" requires verification under compliance rules — change to "Practical" to avoid superlative claims

**Write direction:**
- Buyer guide / roundup format: practical gadgets under £50
- Include only items HCS Gadgets actually sells or could reasonably sell
- Include a checklist format: what to look for in a practical everyday gadget
- CTA: HCS Gadgets practical gadgets range
- Internal links: link to Job 01 (different angle), link to Job 05 (quality signals)
