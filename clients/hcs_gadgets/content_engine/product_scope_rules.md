# HCS Gadgets Product Scope Rules

## Main Rule
Only mention products/categories HCS Gadgets actually sells or may reasonably sell.

## Preferred Product Areas
Focus on:
- home essentials
- storage and organisation
- cleaning accessories
- garden and outdoor products
- BBQ and seasonal products
- useful household gadgets
- kitchen accessories, excluding knives
- lifestyle products
- practical gift products
- small home convenience items
- products from HCS Gadgets owned brands

## Avoid Mentioning
Avoid products that are off-brand, restricted, risky, or not sold by HCS Gadgets, including:
- knives
- Swiss Army knives
- weapons or self-defence products
- alcohol
- nicotine or vaping products
- supplements
- medical products
- prescription products
- gambling products
- adult products
- fake branded/counterfeit products

## Content Rule
If the product is not sold by HCS Gadgets, do not recommend it.

## CTA Rule
CTAs should point toward real HCS-style categories such as home, garden, BBQ, lifestyle, storage, cleaning, and useful gadgets.
