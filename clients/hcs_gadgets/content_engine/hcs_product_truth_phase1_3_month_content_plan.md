## Cadence Rules

- 1 draft every 4 days is the maximum cadence, not a minimum
- If a draft fails validation, pause the queue until it is resolved
- If seasonal opportunity arises mid-quarter, add it to the queue but maintain the 4-day gap
- Do not draft and publish on the same day — Shopify draft must be reviewed before publishing

1. Topic is in the approved queue list
2. Products mentioned are verified in `product_catalog.json`

4. No unsupported product claims
5. Compliance notes reviewed for the specific cluster
6. Word count target: 1,200–1,600 words
7. HTML validator: 42/42 checks must pass
8. Internal links verified before Shopify push
