# Hoverboard Store — UK Product Compliance Rules

Purpose:
Prevent unsafe, illegal, or marketplace-risky claims in blog posts, product copy, Amazon listings, FAQs, metadata, and ads.

## Public-Land Claim Rule

Do not claim or imply that privately owned electric scooters, hoverboards, hoverkarts, kids electric dirt bikes, or similar powered rideables can be used on:

- public roads
- pavements
- footpaths
- cycle lanes
- public parks
- shopping centres
- public land
- school routes
- work commutes

unless verified by current UK law and product category.

## E-Scooter Rule

For privately owned e-scooters:
- Do not say road legal.
- Do not say pavement legal.
- Do not say commute to work/school.
- Do not show or describe public-road use.
- Safer wording: for private land use only, with landowner permission, unless law changes.

Avoid these terms in public product/listing/ad copy:
- commute
- road legal
- pavement legal
- public road
- cycle lane
- ride to work
- ride to school
- street use
- city commuting
- daily commuter

## Hoverboard / Hoverkart Rule

Do not claim hoverboards or hoverkarts are suitable for public roads, pavements, or cycle lanes.

Safer wording:
- suitable for private spaces
- use responsibly
- check local rules
- follow product instructions
- wear protective gear

## Kids Electric Dirt Bike Rule

Do not claim kids electric dirt bikes are for public roads, pavements, or commuting.

Safer wording:
- off-road use
- private land use
- supervised riding
- follow local rules
- use protective gear
- check age/weight/product guidance

## E-Bike Rule

Only use road/cycle-path wording if the product is confirmed to meet UK EAPC rules.

To call an e-bike road-usable as an EAPC, confirm:
- rider age guidance is 14+
- pedal-assist system
- motor power does not exceed UK EAPC limit
- assistance cuts off at 15.5 mph
- not throttle-only beyond permitted limits

If not confirmed, do not make road-use claims.

## Amazon Listing Compliance

For Amazon UK, avoid product attributes or copy that imply public-land use for restricted rideables.

Do not use public-land/commuting claims in:
- is_used_for_what
- is_used_when
- recommended_uses_for_product
- bullets
- product description
- A+ content
- image text
- alt text
- backend keywords

Risky attribute values:
- commuting
- road use
- pavement use
- public road
- city travel
- school commute
- work commute

Safer alternatives:
- leisure riding
- private land use
- supervised outdoor play
- recreational use
- off-road use where appropriate
- gift use
- beginner practice
- family fun

## Metadata Rule

Do not put risky legal/use claims in:
- SEO title
- meta title
- meta description
- URL slug
- H1
- image alt text
- schema description

## Guard Action

If content contains risky public-use claims:
- block publishing
- status = needs_revision
- rewrite using safer wording
