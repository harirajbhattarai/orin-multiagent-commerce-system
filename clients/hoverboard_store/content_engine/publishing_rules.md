
## Visible Body Cleanup Rules

The visible Shopify article body must NEVER show internal metadata labels.

Do not allow these strings inside visible article content:
- SEO Title:
- Meta Title:
- Meta Description:
- URL Slug:
- ORIN
- TOOXIC AISEO
- AISEO
- autonomous SEO intelligence system
- generated by AI
- written by AI

Author rules:
- Shopify author must always be: Hoverboard Store
- Visible byline must always be: By Hoverboard Store
- Authority section must say: Hoverboard Store Team
- Never use TOOXIC AISEO, ORIN, ChatGPT, AISEO, or any AI/system name as public author.

Metadata rules:
- SEO title, meta title, meta description, and slug are internal fields.
- They must not appear visibly in the article body.
- If metadata is included in the HTML file, it must be inside a proper HTML comment only.

## Published Content Protection Rule

ORIN must not edit, overwrite, update, or republish existing published Shopify articles unless the user explicitly requests a specific published article update.

For normal automation:
- Published articles are read-only.
- Published articles are used only for inventory, duplicate checking, cluster planning, and internal-link reference.
- New content must be created as a local HTML file first.
- New content must be published to Shopify as hidden draft only.
- Live publishing requires human approval inside Shopify.

If a topic duplicates a published article:
- Do not update the published article automatically.
- Mark the topic as skipped_duplicate, merge_required, or needs_human_review.
