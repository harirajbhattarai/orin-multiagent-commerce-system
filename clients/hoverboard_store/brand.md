# Hoverboard Store — Brand Context

Tone:
- clear
- trustworthy
- practical
- modern
- helpful

Avoid:
- fake claims
- unsupported “best” claims
- exaggerated safety promises
- generic filler
- keyword stuffing

Trust signals to use where relevant:
- UK-based ecommerce store
- clear product information
- safety-focused buying guidance
- helpful FAQs
- delivery/returns information when provided
